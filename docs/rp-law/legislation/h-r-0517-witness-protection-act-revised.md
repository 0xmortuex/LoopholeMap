## Source Text

CONGRESSIONAL LAW PROPOSALS
H.R. 0517 "Witness Protection Act"

Section I: Short Title

-
- This bill shall be titled the “Witness Protection Act”, it may be cited as such during formal reference

- It may be referred to by code, or common acronym ‘WPA’

Section II. Contents

-
- The Department of Justice will henceforth be required to ensure witnesses called up to the stand face no charges as a result of their testimony.

- A witness in a prior case may not be sued when the prosecutor’s evidence includes any extracts of their statement(s) given under oath. The DoJ will deny all case requests of this manner.

- They will also be protected from non-judicial punishment (e.g. divisional demotion, exile).

- An official witness may still face legal action for lying under oath or giving inappropriate testimony.

Section III. Reasoning

-
- To safeguard witnesses and allow them to testify in a court of law without fearing retaliation, possibly disclosing vital information that may have remained hidden otherwise.

- To protect witnesses who find themselves at risk of harm due to involvement with a case.

Section IV. Waivers

-
- This bill may only be waived by the Chairman in rare exceptions.
