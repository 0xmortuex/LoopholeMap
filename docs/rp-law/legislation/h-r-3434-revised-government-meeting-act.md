TITLE I—GENERAL PROVISIONS
SEC. 101. DEFINITIONS.
- Branches are defined as the following:
- “Executive Branch” is defined as the Executive Office of the President of the United States and White House.
- “Supreme Court of the United States” is defined as the Chief Justice, Deputy Chief Justice(s), and Associate Justice(s).
-  “Department of Justice High Command” is defined as the Deputy Secretary of Justice, Secretary of Justice, and Attorney General of the United States.
-  “United States Congress” is defined as the Deputy President Pro Tempore, President Pro Tempore, Speaker Pro Tempore, Speaker of the United States House of Representatives, Senate & House Minority & Majority Leader & Whip, Representatives, and Senators.
- “Government meeting” refers to a monthly meeting between the branches defined in SEC. 101. DEFINITIONS. (a).
-  “White House Office of Governmental Affairs” is defined as the organiser(s) and presider(s) of the government meeting.
SEC. 102. KEY CONCEPT.
- Accordingly, governmental meetings will be held on the first Saturday or Sunday of each month rather than weekly.

Subtitle A—Preparation
SEC. 111. ATTENDANCE.
- A minimum of one (1) member from each branch must be present in the government meeting.
- All members of the Judicial Branch, Legislative Branch, and Executive Branch, are expected to attend government meetings when they are available.
- Attendance & times must be determined one (1) week prior to the meeting unless replacements are found.
SEC. 112. CANCELLATION.
- A monthly meeting may only be cancelled with valid reasoning presented and a ⅔ majority vote from all parties involved set to attend.
SEC. 113. FAILURE TO ATTEND.
- If any of the aforementioned parties are unable to attend, they must inform the White House Office of Governmental Affairs prior to 24 hours until the scheduled meeting.
- In this instance, a replacement for the absentee must be found. If no one from a branch is able to attend, the meeting will be rescheduled.
- An exemption to informing prior to 24 hours is for last minute emergencies that justify failure to attend. The meeting will go on without them if this occurs.

Subtitle B—Meeting
SEC. 121. LOCATION.
- The meeting must be held in the HQ meeting room on any server. Relevant divisions, sub divisions, units, and/or any authorised organisation(s) may be deployed to guard.
SEC. 122. DISCUSSION.
- In the meeting, the following topics must be discussed:
- Branch performances.
- Executive Branch Proposals & Focuses.
- Legislative Branch Proposals & Focuses.
- Judicial Branch Proposals & Focuses.
- Supreme Court Actions & Plannings.
- Updates on the Department of Justice.
- Legislative Agendas.
- Branch Collaboration.
- Subsection (a) and the subparagraphs (a) contained may be waived by the President of the United States only if other important matters related to the three (3) government branches are required to be discussed.
- If the President is unable to attend, each branch's respective High Command personnel will be able to decide on important matters they need to discuss and if they want to waive the subparagraphs above that are associated with their branch.
- In the case that the High Command members of the three (3) government branches need to waive the subparagraphs listed in subsection (a), more than ½ of those High Command members must agree to do so. One member of High Command may not solely have the power to do so.

Subtitle C—Conclusion
SEC. 131. RECORD.
- Within 48 hours of the conclusion of the meeting, the White House must publish an Electronic Version of the Meeting Transcript & Records to the public.
- Any confidential information must be removed in the document prior to publication while an unedited version may be released for the branches.

Subtitle D—Waivers
SEC. 141. WAIVERS.
- The Revised Government Meeting Act may be waived if granted permission by the Ownership Team.
- The government meeting may be cancelled if CUSA is in an active state of war.
