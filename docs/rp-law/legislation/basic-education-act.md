Date proposed: 02/12/23

Section I: Short Title

-
1.1  This bill shall be titled “Basic Education Act” as its formal reference.

1.2 It’s common acronym may be referred to as “BEA.”

Section II: Contents
-
2.1 The “All Ranks Training Guide” and “Officer Training Guide”  documents should be updated to include a brief summary of the laws and divisions that are in CUSA.

2.2 The said information that should be stated during the “classroom phase” are the following:
- Brief summary of all the divisions and departments.
- Arrest Times and Procedures
- Basic information on the Code of Justice

2.3 If an officer is found not following the regulations mentioned in this act, they may be sanctioned with the appropriate punishment..

Section III: Reasoning
-
3.1 Most players are not aware of the information regarding the divisions and what they do, the proper arrest time procedures, and basic information on the Code of Justice. It’s quite ironic that the “classroom phase” of their training doesn’t actually teach them anything, they are just expected to know the answers to everything.

3.2 While it is their own responsibility to learn all of this by joining the communications (Discord) server, we cannot expect everyone to have access to the server itself due to Discord’s ToS rule.

3.3 This act ensures that everyone is aware of the law and what rights they have, meaning that they are less likely to experience malpractice and malicious acts by a player that knows more than them.

Section IV: Waivers
-
4.1 The act itself may only be waived if the officer in duty himself isn’t in the server and is only training players based on his previous experiences.

Signed,
