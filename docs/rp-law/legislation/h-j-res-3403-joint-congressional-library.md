RESOLUTION
To establish a Congressional Library named “The Library of Congress”.
Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. DEFINITIONS.
- The “Executive Branch” is defined as the following:
- The Department of Justice or “DoJ.”
- The Department of Defense or “DoD.”
-  The Department of the Navy or “DoN.”
-  The Department of the Army or “DoA.”
-  The Department of Homeland Security, or “DHS.”
-  The Executive Office of the President of the United States, which includes:
- The President of the United States or “PoTUS”
- Vice President of the United States or “VPoTUS”
- Department Secretaries.
- White House.
-  Any other Federal Department or Agency that falls under the Executive Office or its respective Executive Departments.
- The “Judicial Branch” is defined as the Supreme Court of the United States.
- “Divisional Command” is defined as any unit of command that falls under the Branches defined in Section 1.
- The “Legislative Branch” is defined as the following:
- The House of Representatives or “HoR.”
- The United States Senate or “USS.”
SECTION 2. PROVISIONS.
- There is hereby established a Congressional Library, to be known as the “Library of Congress,” which shall serve as the official archival repository of publicly available materials of the Federal Government.
- The Library shall archive publicly available documents and/or compositions from all entities defined in Section 1.
- Congress shall be responsible for the collection and administration of materials from the entities defined in Section 1.
-  The Legislative Branch shall be administered separately by each respective Chamber.
- The Library shall be organized into the following categories:
-  Each Chamber will be in charge of managing its own category.
- The Executive Branch will be managed by the Joint Oversight Committee.
-  The Judicial Branch will be managed by the House of Representatives.
-  The Divisional category will be managed by the Senate.
- Each managing Chamber is authorized to adopt rules, procedures, and policies necessary to administer and maintain its respective categories outlined in this section.
- They may also designate which internal entity manages the categories.
- Such power is limited only to the respective Chamber’s categories.
- Nothing in this Resolution shall be construed to require the submission of records, interfere with operational authority, or alter existing powers of any Branch, Department, Agency, or Division.
