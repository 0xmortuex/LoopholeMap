A BILL
To create a standardized list of regulations for divisions to make blacklists known to the public, list any exemptions that may allow divisions to withhold the reason or the blacklist entirely, and to ensure divisions are in compliance with the Code of Justice, Article-06b: Rights of the Citizens, Clause 9.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,

TITLE I—BLACKLIST REGULATIONS
SEC. 101. PUBLIC BLACKLIST REQUIREMENTS.
- Each division within CUSA must have a public blacklist document/registry which shows:
- The Roblox Username of the person blacklisted;
- The Roblox ID of the person blacklisted;
- The Date which they were blacklisted on;
- A short reason why the blacklisted user was blacklisted;
- The length of the blacklist and whether it is appealable or not.
SEC. 102. EXEMPTIONS.
- A division may classify a blacklist or the reason for the blacklist if deemed necessary by the division’s HICOM:
- They must have the approval of the division’s acting director or deputy director for an entire blacklist or its reason to be classified.
- They must not classify a blacklist or its reason unless it violates the privacy of the individual.
- A blacklist and reasons may not be classified unless it threatens national security, in which case it will require an approval from the Deputy Secretary of Homeland Security and above.
- Any reports of misuse or abuse of classifying blacklists unnecessarily will be investigated by the Department of Homeland Security.
