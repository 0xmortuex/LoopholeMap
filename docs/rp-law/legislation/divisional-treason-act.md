Congressional Bill Proposal

Section 1: Title

1.1 This bill shall be cited as “Divisional Treason Act”.

 Section 2: Contents

2.1 This bill shall be applied to all members of CUSA.

2.2 Divisional Treason is when a member intentionally gets themselves exiled so that they can join another division without having to wait for the required time to honorably discharge and without getting a blacklist from that division.

2.3 They break the rules so that the only punishment they receive is an exile and not a blacklist. [Avoiding Desertion punishment]

2.4 This can occur in many different ways such as players wanting to join a different main division whilst they are already in a main division and haven’t met the discharge time requirement, so they intentionally break the rules so they can get exiled from their current main division, and join a different main division.

2.5 Violating this bill can result in serious consequences and is considered as Divisional Treason.
2.6 Committing Divisional Treason will result in a blacklist from that division, which is appealable after 3 months.

Section 3: Reasoning

3.1 They can commit Divisional Treason by trespassing HLS, so that they can receive an exile only and not a blacklist, which is unfair so that is why this bill is created.

3.2 It’s unacceptable how some players are using Divisional Treason to join another division while others are waiting for their discharge time requirement.

3.3 Leaving without serving the required amount of time impacts the division and is considered illegal as they are avoiding a blacklist by getting themselves exiled and not blacklisted.

3.4 The punishment is the same as desertion, so it only makes sense if it’s a 3 month blacklist.

3.5 Violating this bill appears to break many different articles which isn't acceptable at all. Ex: Incompetency [Eligible to sue with the articles the accused broke.]

Section 4: Waivers
