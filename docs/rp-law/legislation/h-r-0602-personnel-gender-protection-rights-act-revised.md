CONGRESSIONAL LAW PROPOSALS
H.R. 0602 "Personnel Gender Protection & Rights Act"

Section I: Short Title

-
- This bill shall be titled the “Gender Protection and Rights Act”, it may be cited as such during formal reference

- It may be referred to by code, or common acronym ‘PGA’

Section II. Contents

-
- Any form of sexual harassment, assault, or discrimination against military personnel based on gender or identified gender shall be prohibited and result in appropriate punishment or civil lawsuit.

- They may be subject to criminal prosecution under the Code of Justice or other applicable laws and face suspension, demotion, termination, etc.

- Military personnel who have had their rights violated have the right to file a complaint(s), sue, or seek appropriate redress.

- Minor violations where judicial action is not required or where the victim has decided it unnecessary may be handled individually by the agents of the Military Education Department or divisional HICOM to discipline a perpetrator without further escalation.

- Military personnel cannot be denied opportunities for career advancement (i.e. promotion, course opportunity) due to their gender.

- Superiors found engaging in discriminatory behavior toward an employee or candidate may face amplified punitive measures for their actions.

- Military personnel cannot be treated differently (with varying degrees of respect, responsibility, etc.) due to their gender.

- A superior cannot provide unequal benefits based on a candidate’s gender identity (e.g. opportunity for promotion, lowered standards or requirements)

Section III. Reasoning

-
- To safeguard the security and rights of all gender identities within the United States Military and ensure all are treated with respect and dignity by establishing rights and sanctions for violations of the abovementioned rights.

- To protect the gender identities of Clockwork’s United States Army from harassment, assault, discrimination, etc., based on their gender identity and to give equal opportunities to various gender identities.

- To promote a secure environment for all gender identities within the military and hold those who violate one’s abovementioned rights accountable for their actions.


Section IV. Waivers

-
- This bill may only be waived by the Chairman in rare exceptions.
