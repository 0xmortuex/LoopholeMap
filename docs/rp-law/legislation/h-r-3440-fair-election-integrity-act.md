TITLE I—FAIR AND FREE ELECTIONS
SEC. 101. APPLICABILITY.
- This Act applies regardless of whether the election is.—:
- Formal or informal.
- Public or internal.
- Temporary or permanent.
- This Act applies to all elections related to ranks or positions across all CUSA.
SEC. 102. PROHIBITED CONDUCT.
- The following actions are strictly prohibited during any election covered under this Act.—:
- Threatening punishment, demotion, removal, exile, strikes,  or any harmful action in general in exchange for a vote.
- Offering rewards, promotions, ranks, money, or favors in general in exchange for a vote.
-  Forcing or pressuring someone to vote a certain way.
-  Soliciting votes using authority, rank, or position.
- Retaliating against a person for how they voted or if they abstained.
-  Coordinating votes through intimidation or abuse of power.
- Misrepresenting rules, consequences, or authority to influence voting.
-  Spreading false or misleading information to influence an election.
-  Coordinating vote manipulation, including vote trading or coercion.
- Participating in an election where the individual has a conflict of interest without proper disclosure and recusal.
-  Attempting to influence an election illegally even if the attempt fails.

Subtitle A—Impartiality and Conflict of Interest
SEC. 111. IMPARTIALITY AND CONFLICT OF INTEREST.
- Any individual who has a conflict of interest in the outcome of an election shall disclose such conflict.
- An individual with a conflict of interest shall recuse themselves from.—:
- Participating in the election.
- Election oversight or administration.
-  Enforcement related to the election.

Subtitle B—Enforcement and Consequences
SEC. 121. ENFORCEMENT.
- Enforcement of this Act shall originate from the oversight or investigative organization of the respective division, department, unit, or body in which the election is conducted.
- If no appropriate investigative or oversight organization exists for the election in question, enforcement responsibility shall fall upon the election administrator.
- An administrator exercising enforcement authority under this section must act impartially and must not have a conflict of interest in the election.
- Any individual or body responsible for hosting, administering, or overseeing an election shall not obstruct, delay, or interfere with enforcement actions under this Act.
- All alleged violations of this Act shall be reviewed fairly, promptly, and without bias.
SEC. 122. CONSEQUENCES.
- Any individual found to have violated this Act shall be subject to consequences, which may include.—:
- Formal warnings.
- Strikes.
-  Removal from the election.
-  Disqualification from holding the contested position.
- Exile or blacklist, where the violation is severe.
-  Any other measures deemed necessary and appropriate.
- Prosecution in a court of law under the Code of Justice.
- The severity of the punishment shall be determined based on.—:
- The nature of the violation.
- The intent of the individual.
-  Whether the violation affected the outcome of the election.
-  Prior offenses, if any.

Subtitle C—Definitions
SEC. 131. DEFINITIONS.
- “ Conflict of Interest “ refers to any personal or professional interests that could affect impartiality in elections.
- “ Vote Solicitation “ refers to any attempt to influence how someone votes.
- “ Coercion “ refers to using threats or pressure to affect voting.
- An election is formal and organized choice by vote of a person for a political office or other position.

Subtitle D—Waivers
SEC. 141. WAIVERS.
- This bill may be waived by the Ownership Team.
