CONGRESSIONAL LAW PROPOSALS
H.R. 1155 "Training & Tryout Limitation Act"

Section I: Short Title

-
- This bill shall be titled the “Training & Tryout Limitation Act”, it may be cited as such during formal reference

- It may be referred to by code, or common acronym ‘TTLA’

- The acronym for ‘Trainings or Tryouts’ is recognised as ‘T/T’

Section II. Contents

-
- The below terms apply to all USM trainings (enlisted or officer) or divisional tryouts (including DoJ)

- T/T must begin at Parade Square, and may not be announced and started at the Inspection or Formation Grounds.

- The maximum amount of attendees in T/T will be 20 as this is the amount of candidate pads in an individual event square.

- If a T/T already has the maximum amount of attendees, the hosts reserve the right to remove excessive attendees at will or call a member of the Military Education Department to do so with the headline command.

- An individual who attempts to rejoin a full T/T after being removed and warned 3 times may be arrested for 100 seconds per the Department of Justice Arrest Procedure for disrupting the event.

Section III. Reasoning

-
- To enhance the roleplay experience as beginning a T/T at grounds meant for practicing formations and inspections is unnecessary and unrealistic.

- To control the number of T/T passers and limit the time of an event as a significant amount of attendees extends it beyond necessity.

Section IV. Waivers

-
- This bill may be waived by the Chairman in rare exceptions.

- Subsections 2.2 and 2.3 may be waived if the T/T is hosted by a member of the Ownership Team.
