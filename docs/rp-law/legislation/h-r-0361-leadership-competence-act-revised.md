CONGRESSIONAL LAW PROPOSALS
H.R. 0361 "Leadership Competence Act"

Section I: Short Title

-
- This bill shall be titled the “Leadership Competence Act”, it may be cited as such during formal reference

- It may be referred to by code, or common acronym ‘LCA’

Section II. Contents

-
- All newly recruited directorial staff, as public servants and appointed heads of divisions will be brought before an Ethics Committee review board

- The Committee will formulate several interview-style questions regarding a candidate’s plans of management, priorities, and attitudes toward misconduct within their division

- They will be analyzed on their responses, arrangements to handle reform, punishment, HICOM misconduct, activity, professionalism, and general plans for the division’s future

- All questions and the candidate’s responses to them must be transcripted and placed into record together with thorough and conclusive background check results

- This interview will be conducted by the Ethics Committee Director or their Assistant, posted for internal voting

- If the Committee determines a candidate’s response or background unsatisfactory, they may advise their removal to the Presidential Office.

- The President has the authority to reject the committee’s conclusion and allow said director to remain in office.

- Candidates who pass this process may remain in office.

- The Committee will not repeat this process for governmental officials such as the Cabinet, the Vice President, and the President.

- Divisional Directors pending review may not take any formal action with their position until qualified by the Ethics Committee

Section III. Reasoning

-
- To ensure newly-appointed divisional directors are capable of holding their position

- To ensure a fair process by an independent Ethics Committee review panel uninfluenced by Congressional command such that findings are disclosed only to authorized individuals and the Presidential Office

- To ensure candidates have satisfactory background

- To implement a qualification for new divisional directors, ensuring they are compliant with laws, regulations, and have the skills necessary to lead their division

Section IV. Waivers

-
- The Committee will not interview a divisional director if they have been chosen for their position by a Chairman.

- This bill may only be waived by the Ownership Team in rare exceptions.
