TITLE I—BILL
SEC. 101. PREAMBLE.
The purpose of this bill is to clearly define and establish how future bills are drafted and outlines the specific usage of following definitions in order to standardize practices across chambers.
        SEC. 102. REPEAL OF CONFLICTING LEGISLATION
- Repeal of the Congressional Codification Act.

        SEC. 104. IMPLEMENTATION
- Implementation Date.
- Transfer of Administrative Functions.
- Enforcement.

Subtitle B—Repeal of Conflicting Legislation
SEC. 111. CONTENT.
The purpose of this subtitle is to repeal conflicting or outdated legislation.

SEC. 112. REPEAL OF THE CONGRESSIONAL CODIFICATION ACT
Upon the enactment of this bill, H.R. 0144 “Congressional Codification Act” shall be repealed in its entirety, and any provisions therein shall no longer have any legal effect.

Subtitle C—Implementation
SEC. 131. CONTENT.
The purpose of this subtitle is to establish a clear date and means of implementation and how it will be enforced.

SEC. 132. IMPLEMENTATION DATE
This Act shall take effect immediately upon passage. All future bills must comply with the standards herein, and the templates provided by the originating chambers leadership.

SEC. 133. TRANSFER OF ADMINISTRATIVE FUNCTIONS
Provisions for administrative functions, such as the definition for bill structure and format, shall be transferred to the internal policies established within their respective congressional chambers. Chamber leadership shall hold the authority to update such internal policies as necessary.

SEC. 134. ENFORCEMENT
The chamber leadership of the originating bill shall be responsible for compliance. Non-compliant bills shall be returned to their sponsor for revision. Only passing compliant bills may be sent forward.
