CONGRESSIONAL LAW PROPOSALS
H.R. 0353 "Presumption of Innocence Act"

Section I: Short Title

-
- This bill shall be titled the “Presumption of Innocence Act”, it may be cited as such during formal reference

- It may be referred to by code, or common acronym ‘PIA’

Section II. Contents

-
- Consistent with the foundational principle of court ‘in dubio pro reo’, any Judge, Jury, or ruling party may not convict a defendant when doubts of their guilt remain.

- The aforementioned maxim will be utilised in all future cases. Therefore, when in doubt, an honor shall rule in the favor of the accused; it is the court’s automatic presumption.

- The accused must be considered innocent until proven guilty.

Section III. Reasoning

-
- To protect the rights of the accused, preventing false sentencing

- To ensure a fair & just legal process.

Section IV. Waivers

-
- This may be waived only by the Ownership team in rare exceptions
