## Source Text

The 19th Congress of Clockwork’s United States of America

CONGRESSIONAL LAW PROPOSALS
         H.R. 0099 “Cuffs Training and Evaluation Act”

Section 1: Short Title

-
1.1  This bill may be referred to as the “Cuffs Training and Evaluation Act”

1.2  This bill may be abbreviated as “CTEA”

Section 2: Contents

-
2.1  This bill will provide the necessary training and evaluation needed for every personnel in all divisions within CUSA who have already attained cuffs and also to those that are about to attain it.

2.2  This bill forces every personnel in every division, prohibiting them from abusing the usage of cuffs.

2.3  This bill will enforce every division to conduct a separate training and evaluation mainly on cuffs.

2.4  Failure to follow this bill places the individual upon potential legal action.

2.5  The targeted individual may press legal charges if they deem necessary.

Section 3: Reasoning

-
3.1  This bill will prevent any abuse of cuffs within CUSA. Numbers in abuse of cuffs have been rising and it has come to a point where several personnels are not logging their arrests.

3.2  This bill will ensure that personnels will be prepared and well equipped with the knowledge on how to carry out arrests.

3.3 This bill will act as a funnel and identify potential offenders of whom may commit crimes by abusing the cuff system.

3.4 By implementing evaluation be conducted, personnels will then be assessed accordingly to identify who may or may not be qualified to attain cuffs.

3.5 Evaluations will be conducted before promoting personnels to a rank where they will attain cuffs.

3.6 Cuffs Training will be conducted at least twice a week for those who possess cuffs in each division.

Section 4: Waivers

-
4.1  This bill may be waived by the Ownership team if they deem necessary.
