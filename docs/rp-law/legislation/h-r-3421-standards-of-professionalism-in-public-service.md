SECTION. 2. DEFINITIONS.
- Government Personnel.—The term “government personnel” refers to—
- Members of Congress and all other personnel officially employed under it.
- This includes personnel that do not qualify as legislators.
- Justices of the Supreme Court and all other personnel officially employed under it.
- This includes personnel that do not have inherent judicial authority.
- Executive officials.
- This includes the presidential Cabinet.
- This includes the President and Vice President of the United States.
- This includes anyone ranked as “Cabinet” in the USM group through a rank equivalent in the executive branch.
- On duty.—Government personnel is considered to be “on duty” when—
- In-game on the USM team.
- In-game on any divisional team.
- In-game on the Cabinet team.
- In Discord channels that include the infix “on-duty” or “on-topic”.
- In Discord channels that are explicitly described to be considered on duty.
- In discord channels that explicitly prohibit unrelated topics from the server or CUSA.
- Off duty.—Government personnel are considered to be “off duty” when—
- In-game on the Citizens team.
- In-game, while acting under the capacity of a community department.
- In-game as a divisional guest.
- In discord channels where personnel are not considered as “on duty” through SECTION 2, (b).
SECTION. 3. SCOPE.
- Title I of this Act shall apply to both on-duty and off duty government personnel.
- Title II of this Act shall apply to on-duty government personnel.
- The provisions of this act shall apply in all CUSA-affiliated games, servers and group chats.
SECTION. 4. WAIVERS.
All uniform regulations enforced through this Act shall not be applicable on divisional teams as long as the division’s uniform policy is being followed, with the exception of divisional guests.

TITLE I—GENERAL PROVISIONS

Subtitle A—Uniform Regulations
SEC. 111. PROFESSIONAL ATTIRE.
- Government personnel must ensure their in-game clothing fulfills the following requirements:
- Must look professional.
- Clothing may not display dirt, blood, or other material that may be considered untidy or unkept.
- Clothing may not be excessively ragged or torn.
- Must conform to standards of decency.
- Clothing must not be too revealing and must cover the majority of the avatar/skin.
- Clothing may not display offensive remarks, slurs, or images.
- Accessories may not enforce a face or any other body part that contradicts the provisions set in SECTION 112.
- Attire provided by the team’s in-game locker may be worn as long as it’s appropriate for the occasion and/or following the team’s guidelines and rules.
SEC. 112. BODY SETTINGS.
- Government personnel must utilize realistic and appropriate body settings, such as
- Realistic skin tone.
- Only the body parts from the “ROBLOX Girl” package or the default body parts may be equipped.
- Heads and Classic Heads may not drastically different from the default roblox head in shape.
- Body parts may not enforce an unrealistic skin tone.
- Body parts may not be too large, too small, or unrealistic.
- Headless and Korblox are allowed to be used.
- Body parts may not enforce a face which contradicts the provisions set in SECTION 112, (a), (iii).
- Professional and realistic face, meaning:
- It may not enforce an unrealistic skin tone;
- It may not have unrealistic facial features;
- Big eyes or smiles are excluded from this as long as the eyes and/or the mouth, apart from their size, can be deemed as realistic in Roblox standards.
- It may not be filthy.

Subtitle B—Speech Regulations
SEC. 121. GOVERNMENT INTEGRITY.
- Officials should not undermine the integrity or legitimacy of their office.
- Speech should not undermine or harm the public trust in democratic processes, whether intentional or negligent, through false statements.
- Officials may not intentionally make any false statements.
- Officials may not encourage or advocate rebellion or overthrow of the government.
- Officials may not misrepresent government policies in bad faith.
SEC. 122. PROFESSIONAL SPEECH.
- Officials must maintain the dignity of their office in all public statements.
- Speech should not be provocative, harassing, or abusive of colleagues, subordinates, or members of the public.
- Private informal conversations are not affected by this.

TITLE II—ON DUTY REGULATIONS
SEC. 201. APPLICABILITY.
This Title applies to all government officials while on duty or acting under official capacity.

Subtitle A—Uniform Regulations
SEC. 211. PROFESSIONAL ATTIRE.
- Officials must wear a suit or another uniform provided through the in-game locker while on duty.
- Attire may not be too flashy or bright neon.
- Attire must be dignified and may not display a childish aesthetic.
- Other attire deemed as at least as professional or formal as a suit.
- Officials may use professional accessories.
- Accessories may not be—:
- too large,
- bright neon or flashy,
- novelty.
- Accessories must be dignified and may not display a childish aesthetic.
- Officials may not use attire that indicates affiliation to an external group or country other than the US.
- Attire provided by the team’s in-game locker may be worn as long as it’s appropriate for the occasion and/or following the team’s guidelines and rules.
SEC. 212. BODY SETTINGS.
Hair must be neat and tidy; it may not be messy and/or cover large parts of the face.

Subtitle B—Speech Regulations
SEC. 221. PROFESSIONAL SPEECH.
- Officials must uphold the usage of proper grammar and abide by the grammatical standards of American English.
- Using single words in the version of a different English may be allowed.
- Officials may use official abbreviations within the rules of English grammar and may not use slang abbreviations or slang deviations from the English language.
- Officials are allowed to use emojis, gifs or stickers as long as they remain within the standards of professionalism and decency.
