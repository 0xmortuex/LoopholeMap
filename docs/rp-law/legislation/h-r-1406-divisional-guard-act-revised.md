## Source Text

CONGRESSIONAL LAW PROPOSALS
H.R. 1406 "Divisional Guard Act"

Section I: Short Title

-
- This bill shall be titled the “Divisional Guard Act”, it may be cited as such during formal reference

- It may be referred to by code, or common acronym ‘DGA’

- The acronym for ‘Trainings or Tryouts’ is recognised as ‘T/T’

Section II. Contents

-
- The below terms shall apply to all Military Education Department law enforcement agents:

- They may request to guard divisional T/T where the hosts do not possess cuffs. Their request may be denied and permission revoked by the host at any time for any reason.

- They may request to guard or supervise United States Military trainings (for both enlisted and officer). This request may be denied and permission revoked by the host at any time for any reason.

- With prior permission from the host, they may utilize their cuffs to arrest individuals who disrupt the aforementioned event for the appropriate amount of time as suggested by the Department of Justice Arrest Procedure document

- They may not request to guard events hosted by federal divisions (Secret Service, Department of Homeland Security, Department of Justice)

Section III. Reasoning

-
- To authorize Military Education Department law enforcement agents to guard and supervise specific T/T while allowing federal divisions legal responsibility to handle their events

- To deter individuals from disrupting events especially when faced with the prospect of jail time


Section IV. Waivers

-
- This bill may be waived by the Chairman and standing divisional HICOM in rare exceptions.
