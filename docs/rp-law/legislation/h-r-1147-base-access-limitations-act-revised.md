CONGRESSIONAL LAW PROPOSALS
H.R. 1147 "Base Access Limiations Act"

Section I: Short Title

-
- This bill shall be titled the “Base Access Limitations Act”, it may be cited as such during formal reference

- It may be referred to by code, or common acronym ‘BALA’

- The formal acronym for “Base Access” is recognized as ‘BA’.

Section II. Contents

-
- Civilians with clearance will be subject to military laws on Fort grounds.

- Civilians with base access may not trespass on divisional property. Civilian trespassers, even with BA, will be immediately arrested or killed per divisional regulations applicable to trespassed property.

- Military Education Department & Military Police personnel reserve the right to arrest BA for various reasons and with valid reasoning.

- BA will be warned twice before being arrested for disrupting CUSA events and/or before being arrested for refusing to provide evidence or respond to an officer questioning their authorization.

- Guests (who have not yet joined the official CUSA Roblox Group) will not be exempt.

- Murder of a BA will be met with immediate arrest.
Section III. Reasoning

-
- Citizens are bound to the law no more than any ordinary military man.

- In previous months, increasing amounts of BA have caused significant amounts of interruptions among official events, ruining the gameplay experience and causing needless trouble.

- Individuals who switch teams cannot remain unpunished for actions committed while under civilian status.

Section IV. Waivers

-
- This Act may be waived by the Group Manager, Founder, or Ownership Team at their discretion.

- Civilians whitelisted in a specific area will not be subject to arrest.

- Subsection 2.3 may be waived for members in possession of the esteemed “Celebrity” rank; though this privilege may be revoked if permissions are abused.
