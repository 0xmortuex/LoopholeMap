IN THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
November 26th, 2025
Mr. SoloxilHavoc submitted the following resolution;

RESOLUTION
To enact a legislative reform, repealing outdated and revised bills, to ensure all bills meet the modern standards.
Resolved, by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. ACTS.
- The following Acts are hereby repealed:
- H.R. 0353 "Presumption of Innocence Act" Revised,
- H.R. 0462 "Recess Inclusion Act" Revised,
- H.R. 0517 "Witness Protection Act" Revised,
- H.R. 1208 "Countersuit Prevention Act" Revised,
- H.R. 1707 "Court Statement Limitation Act",
- H.R. 1147 "Base Access Limitations Act" Revised,
- H.R. 1158 "The Pursuit and Trespass Act" Revised Proposed Amendments,
- H.R. 1705 "Internal Gate Trespassing Act" Revised,
- H.R. 1155 "Training & Tryout Limitation Act" Revised,
- H.R. 8776 Basic Education Act,
- H.R. 0099 Cuff Training and Evaluation Act,
- H.R. 3413 - General Tryout/Examination Fairness Act Revised,
- H.R. 0361 "Leadership Competence Act" Revised,
- H.R. 0602 "Personnel Gender Protection & Rights Act" Revised,
- H.R. 1302 "Superior Sabotage Act" Revised,
- H.R. 1406 "Divisional Guard Act" Revised,
- H.R. 0087 Government Meeting Act
- NSA Accountability Act,
- S. 002 Crimson Raiders Protection Act,
- H.R. 3104 "Multi-Division Quota Relief Act".
- Upon passage of this resolution there shall be a 1 week period where all newly passed laws shall be implemented and divisions and departments shall adjust to the new laws.
SECTION 2. CODE OF JUSTICE.
- The Code of Justice is hereby repealed in its entirety.
- There shall be a 2 week period for cases under the Code of Justice that is being repealed by this resolution, to be initiated. 2 weeks after the passage of this resolution all new cases coming up under the Code of Justice that is being repealed by this resolution, shall be invalidated and all courts shall be adjusted to the new legal standards.
- All cases under the Code of Justice that is being repealed by this resolution that have already been filed before the passage of this resolution or within 2 weeks of the passage of this resolution shall receive the chance to be heard under the legal system that existed immediately before the passage of this resolution or the legal system that was in place when it was filed or accepted until the case is adjourned.
- Such cases shall continue to be subject to appeal and other legal proceedings.
