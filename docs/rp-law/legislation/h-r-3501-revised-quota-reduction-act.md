TITLE I—GENERAL PROVISIONS
SEC. 101. DEFINITIONS.
- Divisions refer to all executive divisions, sub-divisions, and units.
- Departments refer to all executive departments under the federal government.
- Quota only refers to in-game activity measured by minutes or hours and does not include events or other types of quota.
SEC. 102. REDUCTION.
- Individuals that are official members of two or more divisions or departments may request a 20% quota reduction.
- For this clause to apply, individuals must have quota within at least two divisions/departments that they are a member of.
- Official members of the Divisional Support Team may request a 10% quota reduction.
- Official members of the Office of the Records Management Analysts may request a 10% quota reduction.
- Medal of honor recipients may request a 30% quota reduction.
- No division, department, or command authority may grant more than a 40% total quota reduction to an individual due to their position, duties, responsibilities, or circumstances.
- This clause may be waived if an individual’s primary in-game communication methods are involuntarily removed due to mandatory policy changes or platform-wide restrictions beyond the individual’s control.
SEC. 103. APPROVAL.
- Requests for quota reduction must be submitted to the respective High Command which has the authority to approve a quota reduction.
- The respective High Command must verify that an individual meets the requirements listed in Section 102 before approving a quota reduction.
- Approved quota reductions must be immediately revoked upon verifying an individual no longer meets the requirements stated in Section 102.
- The individual who has a reduction is responsible to notify their High Command if a reduction no longer applies.
- If High Command finds out that a reduction no longer applies, they may retroactively apply the quota standards without the reduction that is no longer applicable for the previous weeks in which the reduction did not apply.
- Under no circumstances may divisional or departmental high command exploit loopholes, redefine quotas, or create alternative quota systems to bypass this act.
- Any violation of this subsection shall constitute malfeasance and be subject to investigation and disciplinary action by the appropriate oversight or authority.
SEC. 104. RESTRICTIONS.
- No quota in a singular division, department, or other CUSA entity may exceed 360 minutes.
- Respective High Command may not attempt to bend the quota for individuals with quota reductions approved by this act; the original quota must abide by this section.
