TITLE I—BILL
SEC. 101. PREAMBLE.
The purpose of this bill is to clearly define and establish how members of the Crimson Raiders (CR) and any enforcement department or division may interact with such individuals.
        SEC. 102. RAIDING GUIDELINES
- Raiders Team Authorization.
- Citizen Team Raiding.
- Gamepass and Raiding Compliance.

        SEC. 103. ENFORCEMENT GUIDELINES
- Shoot on Sight Policy.
- Verification of Raider Status.

        SEC. 104. PROTECTIONS AND RESTRICTIONS
- Proximity and Interior Building Arrests.
- Spawn Camping Restrictions.
- Event Restrictions.

        SEC. 105. WAIVERS
- Ownership Waiver.

        SEC. 106. AMENDMENTS
- Removal of Citizen Team Authorization.
- Modification of Verification of Raider Status.
- Removal of Arrest Protocol.

Subtitle A—Raiding Guidelines
SEC. 111. CONTENT.
The purpose of this subtitle is to define proper raiding procedures by members of the Crimson Raiders.

SEC. 112. RAIDERS TEAM AUTHORIZATION
Crimson Raiders are allowed to raid on the Crimson Raiders team as usual.

SEC. 113. CITIZEN TEAM RAIDING
Crimson Raiders raiding on the Citizens team will be subject to arrest for a civilian raiding offense.

SEC. 114. BASE ACCESS RAIDING COMPLIANCE
Crimson Raiders who possess the Base Access game pass must keep their gun out at all times if raiding on the Citizens team to ensure their name tag remains red.

Subtitle B—Enforcement Guidelines
SEC. 121. CONTENT.
The purpose of this subtitle is to define enforcement procedures when interacting with raiders.

SEC. 122. SHOOT ON SIGHT POLICY
Crimson Raiders are designated as shoot-on-sight targets.

SEC. 123. VERIFICATION OF RAIDER STATUS
In the event a Crimson Raider is detained while maintaining proper guidelines in accordance with this bill, the enforcement individual will be subject to cuff abuse punishments.

Subtitle C—Protections and Restrictions
SEC. 131. CONTENT.
The purpose of this subtitle is to establish additional protections and restrictions regarding Crimson Raiders while raiding.

SEC. 132. PROXIMITY AND INTERIOR BUILDING ARRESTS
Crimson Raiders are to be arrested immediately upon entering the interiors of the headquarters, government building, or divisional buildings due to their close proximity to spawn rooms, an action considered spawn camping. They may remain outdoors on divisional and federal properties.

SEC. 133. SPAWN CAMPING RESTRICTIONS
Individuals authorized to act outside of Fort Tedder are restricted from approaching or entering the Crimson Raiders spawn area to prevent spawn camping.

SEC. 134. EVENT RESTRICTIONS
Crimson Raiders are to stay away from any official event that is taking place to prevent disturbance. This includes, but is not limited to, the shooting and killing of individuals at the shooting range during training events.

Subtitle D—Waivers
SEC. 141. CONTENT.
The purpose of this subtitle is to establish any waivers necessary.

SEC. 142. OWNERSHIP WAIVER
Any or all contents of this bill may be waived by the CUSA Ownership Team if deemed necessary.

Subtitle E—Amendments
SEC. 151. CONTENT.
The purpose of this subtitle is to define any amendments that have been made.

SEC. 152. REMOVAL OF CITIZEN TEAM AUTHORIZATION
Citizen Team Authorization has been removed in place of Citizen Team Raiding, which establishes that all civilian raiders, regardless of Crimson Raider membership, are subject to a civilian raiding offense while raiding on the Citizens team.

SEC. 153. MODIFICATION OF VERIFICATION OF RAIDER STATUS
Verification of Raider Status has been modified in accordance with the new raiding guidelines established by the amendment in SEC. 152.

SEC. 154. REMOVAL OF ARREST PROTOCOL
Previously defined Arrest Protocol has been removed in accordance with the new raiding guidelines established by the amendment in SEC. 152.
