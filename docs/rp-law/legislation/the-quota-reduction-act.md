The 20th Congress of Clockwork’s United States of America

        CONGRESSIONAL LAW PROPOSALS
         H.R. 1739 “The Quota Reduction Act”

Section 1: Short Title

-
1.1  This bill may be referred to as the “The Quota Reduction Act”

1.2  This bill may be abbreviated as “QRA”

Section 2: Contents

2.1 As a member in any division the maximum quota that can be given to you by your HI-COM may only be a maximum of 360 minutes.

2.2 This means you will only be required to put in 6 hours of activity per week within your division unless you would like to do more.

2.3 With that being said all divisional members should average a total of 1 hour a day from Sunday to Friday and then reforms would take place on Saturdays.

2.4 This would be the maximum so division HI-COMs may go lower if they please.

Section 3: Reasoning

3.1 This is so many divisional members will not feel any stress while being in their division and enjoy their time.

3.2 This would not lower any activity what so ever as they would still be doing an average of about 1 hour a day and would not affect the division.
