# Amendment to Article 2, § 2 - Presidential Powers and Duties, Clause 3.1.

Source: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1-LzEu4EM4F8z4d2pg4LacfmSpjA1TJ17tsIiZ1CcFtY/edit?usp=sharing
Imported: 2026-07-06

## Source Text

Article: Article 2.
Paragraph: § 2 - Presidential Powers and Duties
Clause: Clause 3.1
Current version:
3.1 Deliver an annual State of the Union speech.
Amendment:
3.1 Deliver a State of the Union speech no less than once per term, within the first three months of the term.

Reasoning: This change ensures and establishes a clear minimum requirement for the President to deliver a State of the Union speech at least once during each term in office and in the first three months. This is done rather than annually, and without any requirements like in the original clause, which makes that clause ineffective. This also ensures that each President and their term has a formal opportunity to address the plan for the nation while allowing greater flexibility in how often the address is delivered.
