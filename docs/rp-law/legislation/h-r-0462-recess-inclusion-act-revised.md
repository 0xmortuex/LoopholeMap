## Source Text

CONGRESSIONAL LAW PROPOSALS
H.R. 0462 "Recess Inclusion Act"

Section I: Short Title

-
- This bill shall be titled the “Recess Inclusion Act”, it may be cited as such during formal reference

- It may be referred to by code, or common acronym ‘RIA’

Section II. Contents

-
- The motion to ‘Recess’ will be recognized as a legal request during any future court procedure. It will be added to the official Department of Justice motions document.

- Recess must be accepted by the judge before in effect. The Judge has the authority to deny said motion if deemed an unnecessary delay.

- It may be used by both the prosecution and defense only during their evidence statement.

- Either side may motion for recess exactly once, whether denied or accepted by the judging body.

- Recesses may last up to a maximum of 10 minutes. Time of recess will be decided by the judge but can be suggested by the requesting side.

Section III. Reasoning

-
- To allow the prosecution or defense the opportunity to rest, convene, and prepare or restructure arguments for use in the evidence statement.

- To allow negotiation between both sides, settlements, or plea discussions

- To allow attorneys time to conduct legal research or review documents & articles of evidence

Section IV. Waivers

-
- This bill may only be waived by the Ownership Team in rare exceptions.
