# Amendment to Article 6, § 2 - Right to a Fair Trial, Clause 7.

Source: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1RX385zekLWcGZ1AnmUYHrI07qL4qUduxZKCcYSz-45s/edit?usp=sharing
Imported: 2026-07-06

## Source Text

Article: Article 6.
Paragraph: § 2 - Right to a Fair Trial
Clause: Clause 7.
Current version:
Right to an impartial judge and jury: The accused shall have the right to be tried before an impartial judge and may choose to have the trial conducted either by a judge alone or by a jury composed of twelve members randomly selected from the community.
Amendment:
Right to an impartial judge and jury: The accused shall have the right to be tried before an impartial judge and may choose to have the trial conducted either by a judge alone or by a jury composed of seven members randomly selected from the community.

Reasoning: Lowering the amount of jurors required for a jury trial to take place will ensure the people’s right to a speedy trial. Cases where jury trials need to be done are very rare because of the amount of people required. Changing it from 12 to 7 will ensure more jury trials take place while still ensuring that a good amount of people are able to judge fairly. Also, in the current state of the game, people are very restricted due to roblox’s chat regulations. This shows that lowering would be better as it makes it easier to find more people in similar age groups.
