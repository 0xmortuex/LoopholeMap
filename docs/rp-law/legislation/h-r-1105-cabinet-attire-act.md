## Source Text

CONGRESSIONAL LAW PROPOSALS
H.R. 1105 "Personnel Firearms Safety & Accountability Act"

Section I: Short Title

-
- This bill shall be titled the “Cabinet Attire Act”, it may be cited as such during formal reference

- It may be referred to by code, or common acronym ‘CAA’

Section II. Contents

-
- All Cabinet on duty (while part of the USM team) must don at least semi-formal, professional attire, regardless of gamepass possession.

- This attire includes but is not limited to store-bought dress suits, coats, vests, shirts, or dresses.

- An outfit deemed unprofessional or disturbing by enforcement officers must be changed immediately.

- Cabinet have the option to wear the ‘Formals’ uniform while on duty.

- The Military Education Department must enforce this bill, ensuring on-duty Cabinet are dressed in semi-formal attire or provided ‘Formals’. An individual who refuses to comply may be arrested for 65 seconds (per the Department of Justice Arrest Procedure) after being given 3 appropriate warnings.

- Members of the Secret Service, Homeland Security, Military Police, and Department of Justice with access to cuffs may execute an arrest on a non-compliant Cabinet(s) in accordance with Subsection 2.3 of the CAA.

Section III. Reasoning

-
- Whether official or purchased, the rank of Cabinet is an esteemed one and a fundamental government official and must therefore appear professional while on the United States Military team.

- Without the implementation of this legislation, the Cabinet would be allowed to walk around in unprofessional attire whereas military officials would be required to wear assigned uniforms, effectively contradicting the roleplay experience.

- To avoid the use of unnecessarily large accessories which obfuscates enforcement officers and prevents them from carrying out their responsibilities, especially as it causes arrest of an individual wearing such accessories to be extremely difficult.

- To lower the chance of Cabinet wearing clothing with bypassed slurs, inappropriate and/or vulgar language, or glitched T-shirts which mar the roleplay experience.

- To ensure government officials look presentable and adept.


Section IV. Waivers

-
- This bill may only be waived by the Ownership Team in rare exceptions.

- Whitelisted individuals will be exempt from the abovementioned attire guidelines.

- A Cabinet member off-duty is free to wear whatever they please.
