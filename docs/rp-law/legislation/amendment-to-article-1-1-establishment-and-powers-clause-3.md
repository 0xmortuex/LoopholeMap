# Amendment to Article 1, § 1 - Establishment and Powers, Clause 3.

Source: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1fyvyIKDddCJouPaVpvXFn4-SVtuCHBnB0LjhJ-gS-XQ/edit?usp=sharing
Imported: 2026-07-06

## Source Text

Article: Article 1.
Paragraph: § 1 - Establishment and Powers.
Clause: Clause 3 in its entirety.
Current version:
3. Each chamber of Congress retains authority to establish procedural rules for legislation, provided such rules preserve the rights of members of congress and prevent concentration of power in any single individual.
Amendment:
3. Each House may determine the Rules of its Proceedings, punish its Members for disorderly Behaviour, and, with the Concurrence of two thirds, expel a Member.

Reasoning: This change increases the self governance potential of Congressional chambers enabling them to change and agree on their procedural rules, which would include the delegation of congressional powers to committees and the ability to determine their own internal rules. This changes one of the fundamental articles (Article I) but does not violate the separations of power in any way since it completely operates within the powers granted to Congress and only enables the chambers to determine how to utilize their powers and to determine on what their own expectations and rules should be.
