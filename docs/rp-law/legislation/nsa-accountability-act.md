Section 1. Title
1.1 This bill shall be cited as the “NSA Accountability Act”

Section 2. Contents

2.1 The purpose of this bill is to provide the CUSA national security agency with a clear message about how to treat members.

2.2 Members investigating then must present the accused with the reasoning behind attacking the member for information.
Section 3. Reasoning

3.1 The reason I believe this is probably one of the most important bills I write up is because right now NSA is running wild with no one to provide checks and balances. It’s important for every division/department to be kept in check so one doesn't grow to be more powerful than the other.

3.2 On multiple accounts NSA has approached me with the most demanding  tone. And that sets the stage for the rest of the conversation. That approach me being demanding, entitled and acting like they need to know congressional stuff when quite frankly it is ran by Zach and Josh.

Section 4. Benefits

4.1 A benefit of having this bill passed is that it will put an end to this behavior. No division should be bullying around directors and its members. Nor quite frankly tormenting CUSA staff cause they’re trying to meet a quota.
4.2 This will allow for the NSA directors to be put in check and allowed to still do their job but with precautions in place to make the game enjoyable for everyone.

Section 5. Waivers

5.1 The only person that can waive this bill once passed is the chairman of CUSA.

5.2 This bill shall go into affect immediately after passed via a public announcement from the POTUS.
