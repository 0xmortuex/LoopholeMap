CONGRESSIONAL LAW PROPOSALS
H.R. 1705 "Internal Gate Trespassing Act"

Section I: Short Title

-
- This bill shall be titled the “Internal Gate Trespassing Act”, it may be cited as such during formal reference

- It may be referred to by code, or common acronym ‘IGTA’

Section II. Contents

-
- Concerning the ongoing development of the West Gate, markers have been added to the interior of the gate to ensure unauthorized enlistees are forbidden from loitering near the entrances and disrupting the responsibilities of the United States Military Police

- These lines shall directly correspond to what minimum rank an individual must have to be allowed into a specific portion of the West Gate

- Therefore, Military Police personnel with access to handcuffs & permission to arrest may do so on USM trespassers below the given rank requirement of an area

- Beyond the ‘Private First Class’ line, enlistees of rank [E2] Private and under may be arrested for 120 seconds by cuff-authorized individuals

- Beyond the ‘Sergeant’ line, enlistees of rank [E4] Corporal and under may be arrested for 120 seconds by cuff-authorized individuals

- Gate section ‘Military Police & Authorized’ can only be accessed by members of the MP & authorized members, anybody not classed as such may be arrested for 120 seconds by cuff-authorized individuals

- Authorized in this scenario is defined as ‘Whitelisted individuals and all ranks above Congress’ including but not limited to Cabinet, President, Staff Team

- Off-Duty Military Police High Command (colonel and above) are also authorized into all internal sections of the West Gate

- By the Founder’s order, a party of 3 or more divisional members led by an officer O1+ may be allowed past this point to patrol out of base; the Military Police may revoke their authority to do so if deemed interfering with their divisional duties

- Military Police and authorised personnel may walk beyond the ‘Do Not Trespass’ line where uncleared citizens (without BA) are forbidden to pass

Section III. Reasoning

-
- To clarify the rank boundaries of the West Gate & give Military Police the authority to arrest those under the minimum rank of a specific gate section for the above-mentioned time.

- To inform the community of the specifics of internal gate trespassing


Section IV. Waivers

-
- This bill may be waived by the Group Manager, Founder of CUSA, or Ownership Team in rare exceptions

- This may be waived if the accused is in possession of the esteemed ‘Celebrity’ role.
