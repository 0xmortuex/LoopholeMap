## Source Text

The 31st Congress of Clockwork’s United States of America

CONGRESSIONAL LAW PROPOSALS
H.R. 3104 "Multi Division Quota Relief Act"

Section I: Short Title
1.1  This bill may be referred to as the “Multi-Division Quota Relief Act”

1.2  This bill may be abbreviated as “MDQRA”

Section II. Contents
2.1 This bill only applies if you are in two or more divisions.

2.2 Divisions refers to all divisions, sub-divisions, and units in all branches.

2.3 If you are in multiple divisions (2+) you may request a 25% quota reduction. Requests must be submitted directly to HICOM and approved by them only.

2.4 No divisional HICOM can deny them their quota reduction. HICOM may only deny a quota reduction request if the member does not meet the criteria outlined in section 2.5.

2.5 HICOM must check if they are actually in two divisions before accepting, and this bill will not count if they leave one division/department.

2.6 Quota only refers to in-game activity and does not include events or other types of quota.

2.7 Divisional HICOM may not try to loophole this bill.

Section III. Reasoning
3.1 To allow members of CUSA to be in multiple divisions without having too much quota in each.

Section IV: Waivers

4.1  This bill may be waived by the Ownership team if they deem fit.
