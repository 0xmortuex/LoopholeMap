## Source Text

CONGRESSIONAL LAW PROPOSALS
H.R. 1302 "Superior Sabotage Act"

Section I: Short Title

-
- This bill shall be titled the “Superior Sabotage Act”, it may be cited as such during formal reference

- It may be referred to by code, or common acronym ‘SSA’

- The formal acronym for ‘Code of Justice’ is recognised as ‘CoJ’

Section II. Contents

-
- ‘Sabotage’ will be added to the CoJ under Subchapter 7: Federal Trial Articles as a chargeable offense that may be applied to a defendant in a court of law.

- It will be defined as ‘the act of hampering, deliberating, subverting or hurting the efforts of another, usually for personal benefit’

Section III. Reasoning

-
- To limit saboteurs from hindering one’s (especially of their superiors) growth or personal efforts to heighten their own advancements within the community such that the potential legal consequence may act as a deterrent.

- To ensure an individual responsible for actions can be held accountable for the specific offense in a court of law


Section IV. Waivers

-
- This bill may be waived by the Chairman in rare exceptions.

- This bill may be waived by the standing Attorney General if they deem it unnecessary or replace it with a different definition.
