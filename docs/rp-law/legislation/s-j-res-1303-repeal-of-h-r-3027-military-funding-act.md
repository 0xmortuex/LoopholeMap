## Source Text

13th Senate
4th Session
S. J. RES. 1303

IN THE SENATE OF THE UNITED STATES
June 23rd, 2026
Mr. Nutnutpro introduced the following resolution; which was referred to the Senate floor.

RESOLUTION
To repeal the entirety of H.R. 3027 "Military Funding Act".
Whereas the MFA doesn’t provide any information or an objective statutory mandate.
Whereas the MFA doesn’t provide any means of enforcement.
Whereas despite its title as the "Military Funding Act," the bill fails to appropriate any actual budgetary funds.
Whereas the MFA contains vague and subjective language, including but not limited to the terms "other events" and  "standardizing new procedures .”
Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United States of America in Congress assembled,
SECTION 1. ACT.
- H.R. 3027 "Military Funding Act" is hereby repealed in its entirety.
- The repeal of this bill won’t affect in any way the CUSA’s legislation due to the previously stated reasons.
