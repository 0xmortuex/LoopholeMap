CONGRESSIONAL LAW PROPOSALS
H.R. 1153 "Slander & Defamation Protection Act"

Section I: Short Title

-
- This bill shall be titled the “Slander & Defamation Protection Act”, it may be cited as such during formal reference

- It may be referred to by code, or common acronym ‘SDPA’

- The acronym for ‘Code of Justice’ is recognised as ‘CoJ’

Section II. Contents

-
- The below terms apply to all CUSA community members excluding the Ownership Team of the specified group.

- An individual who commits progressive slander, libel, defamation, the spread of rumor(s) with ill intentions regarding an accused’s divisional or governmental reputation will be held liable depending on the degree of offense.

- It shall be prohibited for any CUSA community member to commit any of the actions mentioned in Subsection 2.1.1 within experience or in the communications server.

- Members of the community shall be held accountable for slander regardless of tone, including satirical comment

- Any military personnel found in violation of this bill may be subject to criminal prosecution under the Code of Justice or other applicable laws and face suspension, demotion, termination, etc. by the court.
- Military personnel who have had these rights violated have the right to file a complaint(s), sue, or seek appropriate redress via civil lawsuit.

- Minor violations where judicial action is not required or where the victim has decided it unnecessary may be handled individually by the agents of the Military Education Department or divisional HICOM to discipline a perpetrator without further escalation.

- Dissemination of mockery towards an individual will result in additional, heavier punishments depending on origin and reasoning.

Section III. Reasoning

-
- To lower instances of dispute, retaliation, and counteraction and prohibit acts of verbal aggression.

- To impose punitive measures upon violators and maintain a healthy and respectful environment within experience and server.

Section IV. Waivers

-
- This bill may be waived by the Chairman in rare exceptions.
