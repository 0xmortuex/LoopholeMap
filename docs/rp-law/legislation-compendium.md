H.R. 0090 Self Defense Act.pdf
H.R. 0090 Self Defense Act
This bill was suggested by Informationtechyt2. Written by Crazytreegamer5137, and Co-signature by Informationtechyt2. This bill prohibits an individual from being prosecuted and/or punished for acts of self-defense. This bill may be applicable if one has been physically attacked before attacking someone else. The individual must have been fired upon prior to attacking for this bill to be applicable. Self-defense is defined as the act of using lethal or non-lethal force against an individual who is attacking the targeted person. Lethal force is only allowed if the targeted individual has a reasonable fear for their life or if they have previously been attacked with lethal force. Lethal force may only be applied if the targeted individual has been attacked with the same general type of lethal force.
This bill prohibits individuals from being tried if they were attacked with lethal force prior to attacking with lethal force.
First signed to law: 01/02/2024.
